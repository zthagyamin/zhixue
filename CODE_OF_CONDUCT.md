# Community conduct / 社区行为规范

Be respectful, specific and evidence-based. Welcome questions, different experience levels and disagreement about design. Discuss the change or behavior rather than attacking a person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, credential sharing and knowingly publishing private learning data are not accepted. Maintainers may hide/remove violating content, limit participation or close disruptive threads. Apply moderation proportionally and explain the relevant rule where safe.

For a conduct concern, use a sanitized issue requesting private maintainer contact. Do not repeat private material or allegations publicly. Security concerns use the separate private security channel.

请尊重参与者，以具体事实讨论问题。欢迎不同经验、提问和设计分歧；评价改动和行为，不攻击个人。禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视辱骂、人肉搜索、分享凭据或公开私人学习数据。

维护者可按影响隐藏违规内容、限制参与或关闭干扰讨论，并在安全允许时说明依据。行为投诉只公开请求私密联系渠道，不公开复制个人资料或争议细节。
